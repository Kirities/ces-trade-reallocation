# Evidence audit and editorial corrections

[中文版本](../zh-CN/evidence-audit.md)

The supplied 23-page PDF is the conceptual starting point. Its research questions concern trade conflict, Vietnam's adjustment, comparative advantage, investment, and cooperation. The public report develops those questions while correcting the evidence and limits of inference. Page numbers below refer to the supplied PDF. Administrative material and similarity-report pages are not research evidence and are not reproduced.

| Location | Issue in supplied material | Treatment in this project |
|---|---|---|
| pp. 8-9, 12, 16 | 2024 shipments appear as about USD 114.3bn, USD 136.5011bn, and USD 136.6bn | Use one retrieved Census U.S.-import series: **USD 136.3176bn** in 2024. In this vintage, **USD 114.3829bn refers to 2023**. The 136.5011 figure may reflect an earlier vintage; its date is not documented. |
| p. 12 | 2024 U.S. exports and deficit given as USD 13.0441bn and USD 123.4570bn | Retrieved Census values are **USD 13.0683bn** and **USD 123.2493bn**. Treat differences as a reconciliation issue, not evidence of misconduct. |
| pp. 8, 13, 18 | All growth is attributed to the conflict or treated as exact replacement of Chinese output | Replace with descriptive market shares and an exact accounting decomposition. No causal coefficient is estimated. |
| pp. 9-10 | Realized FDI figures are attributed to a generic CEIC FDI page | Do not reuse the series until disbursement, registration, and balance-of-payments net inflow definitions and vintages are reconciled. No FDI estimate is included. |
| pp. 13-14 | Investment relocation is categorically zero-sum, speculative, and without new productive value | Replace with conditional channels: additional capacity, employment, supplier links, uncertainty, and local value capture. Aggregate totals cannot distinguish them. |
| pp. 14, 17 | Specific electronics input and export shares, Samsung production shares, and low productivity growth are insufficiently documented | Omit unverified point estimates and company narratives. Refer to a primary World Bank report for the narrower upgrading discussion. |
| pp. 15-16 | Gross capital formation is used to support capital-stock comparisons; capital tables mix 2021 units and a PWT 10.01 attribution | Withdraw those tables. Investment flows and capital stocks differ; version, variable, price basis, and original rows must be reconciled before reuse. The [official PWT release page](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/pwt-releases/pwt1001?lang=en) is a verification starting point, not a dataset imported here. |
| p. 18 | A “Reciprocal Trade Act” is presented as an operative mechanism; allegations are used to establish widespread circumvention | Omit the unverified legal attribution. Distinguish Section 301 measures from separate origin enforcement and require case-specific evidence for misconduct. |
| pp. 13-14 | Target 17.5 is applied as a general Vietnam FDI criterion | The [UN target](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal17) explicitly refers to least developed countries. Use 17.3 for broader resource mobilization and retain 17.6, 17.9, and 17.10 for relevant cooperation dimensions. |
| pp. 15, 18 | Factor abundance or third-country export gains are used to settle global welfare | Separate comparative advantage, policy-adjusted prices, national income, and global welfare. The model computes only a conditional import-bundle purchasing-power change. |
| pp. 19-20 | Duplicate links, incomplete titles, and broad institutional homepages | Retain a compact, verified bibliography relevant to the implemented analysis. Do not invent missing metadata or reproduce an incomplete citation. |

## Evidence classes

**Observed:** the 45 annual Census partner-year records. **Computed:** shares, indices, compound growth, and decomposition components. **Illustrative:** CES outcomes, tariff wedges, producer-cost shifts, and domestic-value fractions. **Proposed:** product-level causal evaluation and supplier-level upgrading research.

No synthetic historical observations have been added. Scenario values are not empirical measurements, confidence intervals, or forecasts. No claim of peer review or external approval is made. The private source PDF is not required to run any published code; the public extract, manifest, and citations provide the numerical reproduction path.
